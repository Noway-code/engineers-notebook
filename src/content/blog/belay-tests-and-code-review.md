Before a climber leaves the ground, someone else checks their harness, their
knot, and the belay device. Out loud, every time, including when both people
have done it ten thousand times.

## Why the ritual survives

It's cheap, it's fixed, and it's not about competence. Nobody's judgment is
being questioned — the check runs at the same time in the same order whether
you're a beginner or have been climbing for twenty years. That's what makes it
survivable socially, which is what makes it actually happen.

Code review works when it has the same property and stops working when it
doesn't. A checklist applied uniformly is a ritual. A reviewer who looks
closely only at code from people they don't trust yet is doing something else,
and everyone can tell.
