School optimizes for writing code from nothing. Work is almost entirely the
opposite: reading code that exists, written by people who are no longer here,
for reasons that made sense at the time.

## The question that got me furthest

"Why is it like this?" — asked genuinely, not rhetorically.

Every weird thing in a mature codebase is either an accident nobody cleaned up
or a scar from something that went badly wrong. Those two look identical from
the outside and have completely opposite implications for whether you should
touch it. The only way to tell is to ask someone who was there, and mature
teams are usually delighted to be asked.

The corollary: write down the answer where the next person will find it. Half
the confusing code I've met was a reasonable decision that lost its note.
